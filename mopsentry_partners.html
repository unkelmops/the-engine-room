FAQ

Questions?

Here's what potential partners need to know.

How do I get my referral link?

Once your application is approved, MOPSENTRY will provide your unique referral link or partner code.

When do I receive commission?

Commission should be paid according to the official MOPSENTRY Partner Agreement and after any applicable refund or verification period.

Can I promote MOPSENTRY on TikTok?

Yes. Partners can use social platforms where promotional activity is permitted, provided their content follows MOPSENTRY guidelines and applicable platform rules.

Can I make profit guarantees?

No. Partners must not promise guaranteed profits, guaranteed returns or claim that MOPSENTRY cannot lose money.

Will there be a partner dashboard?

Yes. The next stage of the program can include a dashboard showing referrals, qualifying sales and commissions.
